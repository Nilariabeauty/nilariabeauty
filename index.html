Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

At Nilaria Beauty, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, place an order, or contact us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact Nilaria Beauty, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Shipping address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Your order details
  • Information you provide when contacting us

We may request your shipping address before your order is finalized so that we can estimate the applicable shipping cost and arrange delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and confirm your order
  • Communicate with you about your order
  • Estimate and arrange shipping
  • Provide your shipping information to Canada Post when necessary to deliver your order
  • Send you your tracking information
  • Respond to customer service questions
  • Keep appropriate business records
  • Prevent fraud or unauthorized transactions
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

If you choose home pickup instead of shipping, your address will not be required for shipping purposes. Pickup arrangements, including the pickup address and time, will be communicated privately.

3. Payment Information

Nilaria Beauty currently accepts payment by e-transfer. Customers will be provided with the appropriate Nilaria Beauty e-transfer information after their order and total have been confirmed. Nilaria Beauty does not ask customers to provide their online banking passwords, PINs, or other banking login information.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate our business, including with:

  • Canada Post or another delivery service when necessary to ship your order
  • Website or email service providers, if necessary to provide our services
  • Service providers who help us operate our business

Your shipping address will only be shared when necessary to arrange and deliver your order. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights, customers, business, or website.

5. Your Address and Personal Information

Your shipping address and other personal information provided to Nilaria Beauty are private and will not be publicly displayed on our website or shared with other customers. We request your address when necessary to estimate shipping costs and arrange delivery. Your address may be provided to Canada Post or another delivery service when required to ship your order.

6. Website Cookies

Nilaria Beauty does not intentionally use cookies or tracking technologies to collect personal information from website visitors. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain any cookies or tracking technologies that are used.

7. Marketing Communications

If you choose to provide your email address to receive promotional messages from Nilaria Beauty, we may use it to send information about new products, promotions, launches, or other updates. You can request to stop receiving promotional communications at any time.

8. Protection of Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information you provide to us from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss. However, no method of storing or transmitting information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our business, website, services, or legal requirements. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Nilaria Beauty handles your personal information, please contact us through the contact information provided on our website.

Returns & Refunds Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

At Nilaria Beauty, we want you to love your press-on nails. Because our products are personal-use beauty products, we have a limited return policy to protect the health, safety, and hygiene of our customers.

1. Returns

For hygiene and sanitary reasons, opened, worn, applied, or used press-on nails cannot be returned or exchanged.

We may accept returns for products that are:

  • Unused
  • Unworn
  • Unopened
  • In their original packaging
  • In resalable condition

Return requests must be made within 14 days of delivery or pickup.

2. Incorrect or Damaged Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you receive the wrong product, please contact us within 7 days of receiving or picking up your order. Please include:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the product and packaging, when applicable

After reviewing the issue, we may offer a replacement, refund, or another appropriate solution.

3. Nail Size Issues

Customers are responsible for selecting the correct nail size before placing an order. Because our press-on nails are sold in specific size options, we cannot accept returns or provide refunds simply because the selected size does not fit. We recommend carefully reviewing the sizing information provided on our website before ordering.

4. Change-of-Mind Returns

If you change your mind about your purchase, you may request a return within 14 days of delivery or pickup, provided that the product is unopened, unused, unworn, and in its original resalable condition.

For shipped orders, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to an error on our part or another circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

5. Final Sale Items

Items identified as final sale are not eligible for return or refund unless they arrive damaged, defective, or we sent the wrong item.

6. Refunds

Once an approved return is received and inspected, we will notify you whether your refund has been approved. Approved refunds for e-transfer orders will be returned by e-transfer or another appropriate payment method agreed upon by Nilaria Beauty and the customer. Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part or another circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

7. Exchanges

We do not guarantee exchanges for different designs or sizes. If you need a different product, you may return an eligible unopened product according to this policy and place a new order.

8. Home Pickup

Customers may have the option to pick up their order directly from Nilaria Beauty instead of having it shipped, when home pickup is available. Pickup must be arranged in advance. The pickup address and available pickup times will be provided privately after the order has been confirmed. Customers choosing pickup will not be charged shipping fees. For privacy and security reasons, Nilaria Beauty's home address will not be publicly displayed on the website.

9. How to Request a Return

To request a return, please contact Nilaria Beauty through the contact information provided on our website. Please provide:

  • Your full name
  • Order number
  • Reason for the return
  • Photos, if the product is damaged, defective, or incorrect

Do not send a product back until your return request has been approved.

10. Returns That Do Not Meet the Requirements

A return may be refused if the product has been:

  • Opened
  • Worn or applied
  • Used
  • Damaged after delivery or pickup
  • Altered
  • Returned without approval
  • Returned without its original packaging when the packaging is necessary to resell the product

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about a return, refund, damaged product, incorrect order, shipping, or pickup, please contact Nilaria Beauty through our website.

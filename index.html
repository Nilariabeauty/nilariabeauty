How to Apply

  1. Prep natural nails: push cuticles back gently.
  2. Buff the shine off your natural nails and wipe clean with the alcohol prep pad.
  3. Select the correct nail size for each finger.
  4. Apply adhesive, align press-on with your cuticle line, and hold firmly for 30 seconds.
